# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Adding an Xtream playlist is more forgiving: if the address doesn't work as typed, WiseTV also tries it with http/https swapped and without anything after the server name.
- Long server addresses and M3U links now wrap onto several lines, so you can see and edit the whole link, and there's a button to clear the field.
- Clearer messages when the provider's server can't be reached (including the Private DNS setting that can cause it).

## 1.0.0
- First version of WiseTV for Android phones.
- Live TV with a touch TV guide (or a simple channel list), mini player and full-screen player: swipe up or down to change channel.
- Movies and series from your provider, and your Plex libraries, with resume and next-episode autoplay.
- Search across channels, the guide, movies, series and Plex.
- Free channel playlists (Samsung TV Plus, XUMO, Roku Channel, IPTV-org and more) built in.
- Chromecast: cast live TV, movies and Plex to your TV.
- Keeps playing in the background, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
- Updates itself from GitHub releases.
